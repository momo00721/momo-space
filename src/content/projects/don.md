「不可触及」是 OASIS OBJECT 联合陶瓷艺术家王琪举办的陶瓷作品展。展览视觉围绕「观看但不触碰」的核心设定展开，以极简的黑白图像与留白营造距离感与仪式感，邀请观众以目光而非双手感知器物的温度。

"Don't Touch" is a ceramic exhibition jointly presented by OASIS OBJECT and ceramic artist Wang Qi. The exhibition visual revolves around the premise of "look but don't touch," using stark black-and-white imagery and generous white space to create distance and ritual — inviting viewers to feel the warmth of objects through gaze rather than grasp.
