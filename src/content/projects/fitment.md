FITMENT 是专注亲肤体验的肌效贴品牌。品牌视觉以轻盈、透气的质感为目标，标志采用流畅线条勾勒运动与贴合感，包装运用柔和的渐变与触感材质，强化产品舒适、亲肤、可信赖的使用印象。

FITMENT is a kinesiology tape brand focused on skin-friendly comfort. The brand visual aims for a light, breathable feel — the logo uses fluid lines to suggest movement and adhesion, while packaging employs soft gradients and tactile materials to reinforce comfort, gentleness and trust.
