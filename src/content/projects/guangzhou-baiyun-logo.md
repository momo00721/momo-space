广州白云区城市标志征集入选方案，以白云与山形为意象，融合行政区划名称与自然地理特征。标志造型简洁现代，延展至公务用品、数字媒介与公共导视系统，传达开放、包容与活力兼具的区域形象。

A selected entry for the Guangzhou Baiyun District logo competition. The mark combines cloud and mountain imagery, blending the district name with local geography. Clean and contemporary, its applications span official stationery, digital media and public signage — projecting an open, inclusive and energetic civic identity.
