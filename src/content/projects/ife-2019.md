2019年广州国际健身博览会（IFE）是华南地区最具规模的健身行业展会之一。视觉设计以高饱和色彩与动态图形语言传递健身运动的能量感与活力，物料延展包括展会主视觉、观众胸牌、指引系统与现场氛围装置，营造出沉浸式的运动体验空间。

The 2019 IFE Guangzhou is one of South China's largest fitness industry expos. The visual design uses bold, saturated colours and dynamic graphic language to convey energy and vitality. Extended materials include key visuals, visitor badges, wayfinding and on-site installations, creating an immersive fitness experience environment.
