IFE 广州国际健身博览会品牌升级项目。在保留IFE原有品牌认知的基础上，对标志、色彩系统与整体视觉语言进行现代化重塑，建立更清晰、更具延展性的品牌规范，助力展会在竞争激烈的健身会展市场中脱颖而出。

IFE Guangzhou International Fitness Expo brand upgrade. Building on existing brand recognition, the logo, colour system and overall visual language were modernised and refined. Clearer, more flexible brand guidelines were established to help the expo stand out in an increasingly competitive fitness exhibition market.
