2024年广州国际健身博览会（IFE）主视觉设计。延续展会品牌的视觉基因，以更具冲击力的图形语言与色彩体系回应后疫情时代的健身热潮，物料涵盖展会主视觉、线上推广、现场导视与观众物料。

Key visual design for IFE Guangzhou 2024. Building on the expo's visual DNA, a bolder graphic language and colour system respond to the post-pandemic fitness boom. Deliverables include the main expo visual, online promotion, on-site wayfinding and visitor materials.
