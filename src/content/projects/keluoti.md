可洛缇是主打天然植物成分的润肤露品牌，以四季主题划分产品线。包装设计通过柔和的插画笔触与季节性的色彩渐变区分春、夏、秋、冬四款产品，瓶型线条温润，整体调性清新自然，呼应品牌温和亲肤的核心主张。

Keluoti is a botanical lotion brand organised around the four seasons. The packaging differentiates spring, summer, autumn and winter variants through soft illustrative strokes and seasonal colour gradients. Gentle bottle contours and a fresh, natural tone echo the brand's gentle, skin-friendly core proposition.
