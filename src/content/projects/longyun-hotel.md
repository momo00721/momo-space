龙运大酒店视觉系统升级项目，在保留酒店原有品牌资产的基础上进行优化与现代化。新的标志在传承经典结构的同时提升精致度，VI系统延展至房卡、信笺、餐饮物料与环境导视，强化高端商务酒店的品质感知。

A visual system upgrade for Longyun Hotel, optimising and modernising while preserving existing brand equity. The refreshed logo refines the classic structure, and the VI system extends to room cards, stationery, dining collateral and environmental signage — reinforcing the perception of a premium business hotel.
