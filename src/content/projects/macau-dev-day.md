2020年澳门发展关注日是由澳门政府支持的城市发展主题论坛。活动视觉设计以「连接」与「成长」为关键词，通过重叠的几何形态与澳门城市色彩的抽象提取，构建兼具官方属性与当代审美的活动形象。

The 2020 Macau Development Day is a government-supported urban development forum. The event visual design keys on "connection" and "growth," using overlapping geometric forms and abstracted Macau city colours to build an event identity that balances official gravitas with contemporary aesthetics.
