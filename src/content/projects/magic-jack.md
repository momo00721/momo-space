神奇的杰克是一个面向年轻家庭的教育培训品牌。2020–2021年度推广手册以品牌IP角色「杰克」为主角，通过场景化的插画叙事与活泼的版式设计，将课程产品以故事化的方式呈现，增强品牌的亲和力与记忆点。

Magic Jack is an education and training brand for young families. The 2020–2021 promotion brochure stars the brand's IP character "Jack," using scenario-based illustrated storytelling and playful layout design to present course offerings as narratives — boosting brand approachability and memorability.
