MANKE 曼刻是一个潮流健身装备品牌。品牌视觉以力量与速度感为驱动，标志采用粗犷有力的字体设计，黑金配色贯穿产品包装与线上视觉，面向追求个性与品质的新一代健身爱好者。

MANKE is a trendy fitness equipment and apparel brand. Driven by a sense of power and speed, the logo uses bold, forceful typography with a black-and-gold palette running through product packaging and online visuals — speaking to a new generation of fitness enthusiasts who value personality and quality.
