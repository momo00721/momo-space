Mutalk 妙橘说是一个以佛手柑文化为灵感的新一代健康食品品牌。视觉形象以佛手柑的独特造型为切入点，配色清新活泼，包装设计结合插画与轻松的品牌语调，与年轻人建立自然、有趣的沟通。

Mutalk is a next-gen health food brand inspired by Buddha's hand fruit (bergamot) culture. The visual identity takes the fruit's distinctive silhouette as its entry point, with a fresh and playful colour palette. Packaging combines illustration with a lighthearted brand voice to connect naturally with younger audiences.
