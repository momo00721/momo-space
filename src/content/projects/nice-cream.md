Brand identity and packaging design for NICE CREAM, a premium ice cream brand based in Shanghai. The visual system explores the contrast between the organic qualities of natural ingredients and the structured geometry of modern packaging.

奈似雪糕品牌视觉识别与包装设计。视觉系统探索了天然成分的有机质感与现代包装结构化几何之间的对比关系。
