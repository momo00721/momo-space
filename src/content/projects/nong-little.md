濃一点是一个新派柠檬茶品牌，主打美式风格的冰柠茶饮品。品牌视觉以浓郁的色彩和大胆的字体冲突出挑，在传统茶饮与新式潮流之间找到平衡，店面物料与包装均强调即时的视觉冲击力与社交传播属性。

Nong Little is a new-style lemon tea brand serving American-style iced lemon tea. The brand visual breaks through with rich colour saturation and bold typographic contrast, striking a balance between traditional tea culture and modern street trends. Store collateral and packaging both prioritise instant visual impact and social-media shareability.
