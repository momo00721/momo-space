庆溢照明总部位于中山，是一家集研发、制造、销售于一体的照明企业。本次形象升级以简化现有标志为切入点，保留品牌识别基因的同时提升现代感与精致度，并将新视觉系统延展至产品包装、宣传物料与展厅空间，助力品牌从制造形象向品质品牌转型。

Headquartered in Zhongshan, Qingyi Lighting integrates R&D, manufacturing and sales. This rebrand simplifies the existing logo while preserving brand recognition, elevating its modern and refined appeal. The new visual system extends to product packaging, marketing collateral and showroom spaces, supporting the brand's shift from manufacturer to quality-driven brand.
