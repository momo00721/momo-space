R 是一个强调极简与个性表达的设计师品牌。视觉系统以单字母「R」为唯一识别核心，通过材质、比例与空间关系的反复推敲，在极度克制的视觉语言中寻找丰富性，诠释「少即是多」的品牌哲学。

R is a designer brand emphasing minimalism and individual expression. The visual system uses the single letter 「R」 as its sole identifier, finding richness within an extremely restrained visual language through careful attention to material, proportion and spatial relationships — a statement in "less is more."
