SIC新加坡创新中心是一个连接科技与商业的国际创新平台。标志设计以简洁的几何形态传达连接、协作与前瞻性，蓝色主调传递专业与信赖感，延展系统覆盖办公事务、数字媒介与空间导视，呈现一个面向未来的创新枢纽形象。

SIC Singapore Innovation Centre is an international platform bridging technology and business. The logo uses clean geometric forms to convey connection, collaboration and forward-thinking vision. A blue palette signals professionalism and trust, while the identity system spans stationery, digital media and spatial signage — shaping the image of a future-ready innovation hub.
