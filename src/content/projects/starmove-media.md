和悦星动是一家专注少儿演艺培训与童星经纪的MCN机构，品牌CEO为90后女性团队，定位中高端少儿演艺教育。LOGO以品牌首字母"S"融合星星元素，寓意"movie star"与行业属性，整体采用轻奢风格，以香槟金为主色调，传达专业、高端与信赖感。

Starmove Media is a children's talent training and MCN agency founded by a young female-led team, positioned in the mid-to-high-end children's performing arts education market. The logo fuses the initial "S" with a star motif — referencing "movie star" and the entertainment industry. A light-luxury aesthetic with champagne gold accents conveys professionalism, premium quality and trust.
