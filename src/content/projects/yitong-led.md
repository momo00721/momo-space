亿通LED专注于LED照明产品的研发与销售。包装设计以产品线为出发点，运用统一的信息层级与色彩编码体系区分不同系列，在保持品牌识别一致性的同时提升货架辨识度，兼顾工业品的功能表达与零售场景的视觉吸引力。

Yitong LED specialises in LED lighting products. The packaging design builds from the product line, using a unified information hierarchy and colour-coding system to distinguish series. It maintains brand consistency while boosting shelf presence — balancing functional clarity for industrial products with visual appeal in retail settings.
